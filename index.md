## 1. Spin Hamiltonian, magnetic anisotropy and excitation spectrum in altermagnetic $α$-MnTe from first principles

- **提交日期**：2026-11-23
- **作者**：Matteo Costa, Dario Fiore Mosca, Cesare Franchini, Leonid V. Pourovskii
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31548v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31548v1)

### 摘要
The hexagonal altermagnet $α$-MnTe combines a near-room-temperature collinear order with a strongly spin-split electronic structure, yet the microscopic origin of its easy-plane anisotropy, its zone-center magnon gap and its anomalously large magnetostriction have remained unexplained. We derive the complete low-energy magnetic Hamiltonian of $α$-MnTe from first principles, combining density functional theory and dynamical mean-field theory with a force-theorem evaluation of the full anisotropic intersite exchange, single-ion terms and classical dipolar couplings. The isotropic exchange, including the altermagnetic splitting of the tenth-neighbor coupling, agrees with inelastic neutron scattering data. Contrary to the common single-ion picture, we find that the easy-plane anisotropy and the magnon gap at $Γ$ are governed by classical dipole-dipole interactions, which enhance the zone-center magnon splitting threefold leading to quantitative agreement with experiment, while the Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya term is irrelevant despite its size. The same Hamiltonian reproduces the chiral magnon splitting along antinodal directions, mirroring the electronic spin splitting, and quantitatively accounts for the giant magnetovolume coupling of $α$-MnTe through the volume dependence of superexchange.

## 2. Competing magnetic correlations in the square-lattice Hubbard model with spin-dependent nearest-neighbor and next-nearest-neighbor hopping

- **提交日期**：2026-11-21
- **作者**：Rana Imran Mushtaq, Ji Liu, Yinlong Li, Wing Chi Yu, Xiaosen Yang, Ho-Kin Tang
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28593v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28593v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnets have spin-split bands without net magnetization. The splitting can come from a spin-dependent nearest-neighbor (NN) hopping anisotropy $t\_A$ or from a spin-dependent next-nearest-neighbor (NNN) hopping $t'$. Both change sign under a $90^\circ$ rotation and are present together when the lattice has no mirror planes, yet they have so far been studied only separately. Here we study the two-dimensional square-lattice Hubbard model with both $t\_A$ and $t'$, using constrained-path quantum Monte Carlo at $U=4t$ and scanning the electron filling $n$, $t\_A$ and $t'$. We find four types of magnetic correlations: Néel, stripe, diagonal, and spiral spin-density-wave. The phase diagram depends on both hoppings together. Diagonal correlations require finite $t\_A$ and are suppressed by $t'$. Stripe correlations appear only once $t'$ is large enough to overcome the diagonal tendency set by $t\_A$. Néel correlations survive at large $t\_A$ and large $t'$, where the NNN model alone gives stripe or spiral correlations. The noninteracting susceptibility matches the dominant wavevector at small $t'$, but not the Néel correlations at $t'\geq0.3$, which come from the interaction. Finite-size scaling with three lattice sizes is consistent with long-range order only near half filling; at the stripe, spiral and diagonal points we tested, the structure factor per site extrapolates to zero. The joint behavior persists up to $U=6t$.

## 3. Growth and anisotropic directional dependent transport properties of Altermagnetic CrSb

- **提交日期**：2026-11-20
- **作者**：Resham Babu Regmi, Hari Bhandari, Sk Jamaluddin, Abhijeet Nayak, Ashok Chaudhary, Mohamed El Gazzah, Alex T. Sheardy, Maksym Zhukovskyi, Allen G. Oliver, Nirmal J. Ghimire
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27072v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27072v1)

### 摘要
CrSb is a metallic altermagnet exhibiting large momentum-dependent spin splitting and antifer- romagnetic ordering well above room temperature. Although its electronic and magnetotransport properties have attracted considerable attention, a systematic comparison of crystals prepared us- ing different growth methods remains limited. Here we investigate the structural, magnetic, and anisotropic transport properties of CrSb single crystals grown by Sb(self)-flux, Bi flux, and chem- ical vapor transport (CVT). Each of these growth reveals distinct crystal morphologies. Magne- tization measurements exhibit broadly consistent antiferromagnetic responses, with an additional low-temperature contribution observed in Bi-flux-grown crystals. All samples display metallic resis- tivity and positive, nonsaturating magnetoresistance up to 9 T. Sb-flux- and CVT-grown crystals exhibit comparable residual resistivity ratios and magnetoresistance, whereas Bi-flux-grown crys- tals show enhanced residual resistivity and a substantially reduced magnetoresistance, consistent with stronger carrier scattering. Hall measurements reveal a pronounced dependence on the field- current configuration, including nonlinear field dependence and opposite Hall polarities for different crystallographic orientations. These results establish the reproducibility of anisotropic magneto- transport across distinct growth routes while highlighting the sensitivity of carrier scattering to crystal-growth conditions. Our findings provide a comparative framework for selecting CrSb single crystals for orientation-dependent transport and spintronic investigations.

### 摘要
Understanding spin dependent transport in altermagnets is important for incorporating them in next generation spintronic technologies. ## 5. Surface-bulk magnetic dichotomy in intercalated transition metal dichalcogenides

- **提交日期**：2026-11-15
- **作者**：Ke Liao, Wenxin Li, Kelly Dai, Ariel Mazor, Shan Wu, Yu He, Tianyu Zhu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20123v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20123v1)

### 摘要
Magnetically intercalated transition metal dichalcogenides are layered materials with highly tunable magnetic orders and unconventional transport properties. Here we report a surface-bulk magnetic dichotomy in $\mathrm{M}\_{1/3}\mathrm{T}\mathrm{X}\_{2}$, in which the surface and bulk favor distinct magnetic orders. In $\mathrm{Fe}\_{1/3}\mathrm{Nb}\mathrm{S}\_{2}$, angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) results are reproduced by an A-type antiferromagnetic electronic structure, while neutron scattering and bulk DFT+$U$ calculations identify stripe order as the bulk ground state. DFT+$U$ slab calculations resolve this contradiction by showing that relaxation of an Fe-terminated surface reverses the magnetic preference from stripe to A-type order. The same dichotomy recovers the $K$-centered Fermi-surface pockets that are observed in $\mathrm{Co}\_{1/3}\mathrm{Nb}\mathrm{S}\_{2}$ and $\mathrm{Co}\_{1/3}\mathrm{Ta}\mathrm{S}\_{2}$ but absent from previous bulk calculations. Notably, the surface-stabilized magnetic phase of $\mathrm{Fe}\_{1/3}\mathrm{Nb}\mathrm{S}\_{2}$ carries nonrelativistic spin splitting and can exhibit altermagnetic character. These results caution against the general association of ARPES signals with bulk magnetic states in these systems, and suggest surface-bulk magnetic dichotomy as a promising route to realize magnetic phases, including altermagnetism, that are not energetically favored in the bulk.

## 6. Spectroscopic evidence for unconventional surface magnetism in the Kondo insulator FeSb${}\_2$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-15
- **作者**：Ke-Jun Xu, Erin E. Fleck Jonathan A. Sobota, Nhat Huy M. Tran, Shuyi Li, Youzhe Chen, Soonsang Huh, Takeshi Kondo, Makoto Hashimoto, Dong-Hui Lu, Zhi-Xun Shen, Dung-Hai Lee, Chunjing Jia, Robert J. Birgeneau
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19804v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19804v1)

### 摘要
Kondo insulators have a non-magnetic bulk due to many-body screening of local moment by conduction electrons. However, the surface of these systems has reduced density of itinerant electrons and may experience a Kondo breakdown, leading to recovery of non-zero local moments and possible magnetic order. Here we use angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy to investigate the (010) surface of a $d$-$p$ electron Kondo insulator FeSb${}\_2$, which is predicted to host a bulk $d$-wave altermagnetic state in the absence of Kondo screening. We find anisotropic splitting on the surface states with a maximum in the $k\_z$ direction and a minimum in the $k\_x$ direction. The splitting is apparently suppressed with increasing temperature or with Te doping, suggesting that the splitting arises from a magnetic order rather than Rashba effects. Considering the proximity of the bulk to a $d$-wave altermagnetic state and considering the symmetry of the crystal on the surface, we postulate that our observations may be consistent with an emergent surface altermagnetic order. Such an unconventional 2-dimensional magnetic order naturally hosted on an insulating, non-magnet bulk substrate may offer a promising platform for versatile applications.

## 7. Controlled Magnon Chirality and Its Reversal by Anisotropy in Altermagnetic FeF${}\_2$

- **提交日期**：2026-11-13
- **作者**：E. M. Smith, V. O. Garlea, D. Lederman, J. M. Tranquada, I. A. Zaliznyak, J. Sears
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18182v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18182v1)

### 摘要
In altermagnets, the exchange symmetry of two mutually compensating magnetic sublattices permits spin-polarized electronic bands and chirality-split magnon bands. Because these effects have opposite signs in domains related by time reversal, magnetic-domain control is essential for their observation and utilization. Using half-polarized neutron diffraction, we demonstrate that we can control magnetic domain imbalance in a single crystal of FeF${}\_2$ by field cooling, and we relate this response to the established piezomagnetism in this material. Our half-polarized inelastic neutron scattering measurements directly resolve the altermagnetic splitting of chiral magnon bands. Strikingly, we observe a clear reversal of chirality near $\mathbf{Q}=(0.5,0.5,1)$, where the altermagnetic exchange splitting vanishes and no chirality is expected in a nonrelativistic exchange model. This reversal of the chirality is well explained by the presence of a transverse component of the single-ion anisotropy tensor, which rotates between Fe sublattices related by the crystal's altermagnetic $4\_2$ screw symmetry. These results allow us to resolve fine features of the spin Hamiltonian for FeF${}\_2$ and show how symmetry-governed relativistic anisotropy can reshape altermagnetic spin dynamics, as well as providing an avenue for control of magnetic domains and the net chiral response.

## 8. Narrow-Band Magnetism Allowed by Quantum Geometry: Altermagnetism and Beyond

- **提交日期**：2026-11-12
- **作者**：Minxuan Wang, Pei-Han Lin, Yang Ge, Jiabin Yu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17414v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17414v1)

### 摘要
Quantum geometry plays a central role in shaping correlated phases in narrow bands, yet its influence on the competition between distinct magnetic orders, especially the emergence of altermagnetism, remains largely unexplored. To address this question, we study the quadrupole insulator model with strictly onsite generalized Hubbard-Kanamori interactions, in which both Néel antiferromagnetism and altermagnetism are symmetry-permitted. Depending on the parameters, the isolated narrow lowest band of the model can either be obstructed atomic with strong quantum geometry or trivial atomic with weak quantum geometry, while the dispersion is identical in the two cases. By partially filling the lowest band, our Hartree-Fock calculations show only conventional ferromagnetic phases for the weak quantum geometric case, while we find two collinear altermagnetic phases, as well as nematic phases, for the strong quantum geometric case. Such a dramatic difference can be understood by the distinct characters of the projected interaction: the projected interactions acquire sizable longer-range channels only for the strong quantum geometric case, while they are dominated by the on-site Hubbard channel in the weak quantum geometric case. These results establish the essential role of quantum geometry for altermagnetism to emerge from interacting narrow bands where crystalline symmetries cannot sufficiently constrain the magnetic order.

## 9. Noise-Protected Superconducting Qudits from Higher-Harmonic Josephson Junctions

- **提交日期**：2026-11-12
- **作者**：Hamed Vakili
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16563v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16563v1)

### 摘要
Qudits, which generalize qubits to $d$ levels, store more information per circuit element than qubits. As with qubits, however, their sensitivity to noise must be overcome before they can be used for fault-tolerant quantum computing. Here we show that, by exploiting the second harmonic of the current--phase relation, a superconducting loop of three Josephson junctions can host qutrits strongly protected against relaxation, while dephasing limits their coherence. When the second harmonic is positive, a simple loop with a small asymmetry between the junctions provides a tunable, symmetry-protected qutrit. When it is negative, a protected qutrit can be realized by adding a small symmetry-breaking term, such as a weak Josephson diode effect. The two designs involve different trade-offs: the first has an exact charge selection rule, whereas the second is nearly insensitive to charge noise and its quasiparticle errors can be corrected. The preferable choice therefore depends on the materials used. Both require a sizable second harmonic to begin with, and we identify altermagnetic Josephson junctions as a candidate platform. Finally, microscopic calculations show that a gate voltage selects the sign of the second harmonic in these junctions, although gate-voltage noise then becomes the leading source of dephasing. These findings establish the sign of the second harmonic as a design principle for protected superconducting qudits.

## 11. Higher-order topology induced by altermagnetism in quasicrystals

- **提交日期**：2026-11-11
- **作者**：Xingmin Huo, Xingchuan Zhu, Xingyu Ma, Shiping Feng, Huaiming Guo
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15139v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15139v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnetism (AM), a novel magnetic phase featuring zero net magnetization and momentum-dependent spin splitting, provides a compelling platform for exploring exotic quantum phenomena. While recent studies have extended the concept of AM to quasicrystals, the momentum-dependent spin splitting in the absence of translational symmetry remains inadequately understood. Here, we investigate the emergence of higher-order topological phases (HOTPs) in Thue-Morse and Ammann-Beenker quasicrystals, driven by the coupling between $d$-wave quasicrystal AM orders and a two-dimensional topological insulator. We reveal that the $d\_{x^2-y^2}$-wave AM exhibits a ferromagnetism-like aspect in opening edge gaps and inducing zero-energy higher-order topological corner states. In contrast, conventional antiferromagnetic and $d\_{xy}$-wave AM configurations are unable to induce the higher-order topological gaps. These findings highlight the pivotal role of altermagnetic symmetry and its alignment with lattice corners in the realization of HOTPs and establish that the emergence of HOTPs can serve as a signature for identifying altermagnetic orders in aperiodic systems.

- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13579v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13579v1)

### 摘要
RuO${}\_2$, once the flagship altermagnetic candidate, is now believed to be nonmagnetic in the bulk, yet weak, static and spatially inhomogeneous magnetism emerges at its surfaces and interfaces. Here we address these questions at the Nb/RuO${}\_2$ interface using a superconducting spin switch. A GdN/Nb/RuO${}\_2$ trilayer allows interfacial exchange-field alignment to tune the superconducting transition temperature of Nb, probing interfacial magnetism inaccessible to conventional magnetometry. ### 摘要
Zeeman fields are usually required for realizing and manipulating Majorana corner modes at the cost of suppressed superconducting pairing. Altermagnetism offers a new route toward realizing Majorana corner modes under zero net external magnetic fields. Here we propose a theoretical scheme to host Majorana corner modes in a honeycomb-lattice altermagnet-superconductor heterostructure. ## 14. LaQX: A JAX Toolkit for Neural Quantum State Simulations of Lattice Quantum Many-Body Models

- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04333v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04333v1)

### 摘要
Neural quantum states provide accurate variational representations for interacting lattice fermions and spins. ## 15. Resolving magnetic order and dynamics with polarized neutron scattering: a practical guide to longitudinal polarization analysis

- **提交日期**：2026-11-02
- **作者**：Sijie Xu, Pengcheng Dai
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04050v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04050v1)

### 摘要
Neutron scattering provides microscopic information on magnetic order and spin dynamics. Most experiments, however, use unpolarized neutrons and do not fully exploit the information carried by the neutron spin. Longitudinal polarization analysis (LPA), established in the pioneering work of Moon, Riste, and Koehler at Oak Ridge National Laboratory in the late 1960s, separates magnetic and nonmagnetic scattering contributions through their different effects on the neutron spin. In this review, we illustrate how LPA can distinguish weak magnetic scattering from nonmagnetic contributions, constrain small moment components, and resolve the spin-space anisotropy of magnetic excitations. We introduce the formalism connecting measured polarization channels to magnetic quantities and discuss scattering geometry, crystallographic projections, and corrections for finite polarization. Our aim is to provide a practical guide for neutron scatterers using LPA to determine magnetic structures and characterize spin excitations in quantum materials. We conclude with opportunities in altermagnets, unconventional superconductors, and kagome magnets.

## 16. X-ray magnetic circular dichroism in altermagnets: insights from symmetry

- **提交日期**：2026-10-31
- **作者**：Hana Schiff, Ishmam Nihal, John Vinson, Robin Msiska, Judit Romhányi, David Lederman, Christoph Klewe, Sujoy Roy, Eric E. Fullerton
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00622v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00622v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnets are a unique subset of antiferromagnets possessing ferromagnet-like responses that are odd in the Néel vector, making X-ray magnetic circular dichroism (XMCD) a natural probe of ground state altermagnetic order. Because the relativistic ingredient of XMCD at the $L$-edge is supplied predominantly by core-level spin--orbit coupling (SOC), the dichroic signal can reflect the non-relativistic order of the valence electrons. Here we develop a complete symmetry framework for XMCD in altermagnets (AMs) preserving the crystalline unit cell. We tabulate the symmetry-allowed optical Hall vector, anisotropic magnetic dipole (AMD), and underlying octupolar components for all magnetic point groups, thereby identifying the experimental geometries that maximize the XMCD signal. We show that only twelve magnetic point groups, none with a rotation axis of order three or higher, are compatible with both altermagnetism and finite XMCD. By extending the Landau theory of collinear AMs, we connect these relativistic constraints to the spin--orbit-free order. We show that the AMD is forced to vanish at zero valence SOC in conventional antiferromagnets, and that every AM sublattice structure allows the Hall vector and AMD at linear or cubic order in the Néel vector; whether the resulting XMCD is nonzero depends on the Néel vector orientation at finite SOC. The Landau theory thus fixes the Néel-vector dependence of the XMCD for all AM candidates, and separates $d$-wave AMs, whose signal is inherited from a non-relativistic AMD, from $g$- and $i$-wave AMs, whose signal requires valence SOC or core--valence multiplet effects. We illustrate these predictions with first-principles Bethe--Salpeter calculations of $L$-edge XMCD for five representative altermagnetic materials and one conventional antiferromagnet.

## 17. Meta-Altermagnetism and Enhanced Spin-Splitting

- **提交日期**：2026-10-27
- **作者**：Bogeng Wen, Hae-Young Kee
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37754v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37754v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnets are collinear magnets with zero net magnetization and spin-split electronic bands. Here, we explore whether altermagnetism can exhibit a discontinuous jump, a phenomenon we refer to as meta-altermagnetism. We show that meta-altermagnetism can emerge in 2D systems when magnetic-order-induced band reconstruction drives spin-split van Hove singularities (vHSs) across the Fermi level. Using mean-field theory for an extended $t$-$J$ model on a two-sublattice square lattice including anisotropic hopping integral, we find that a first-order meta-altermagnetic transition produces an intermediate altermagnetic regime with reduced staggered magnetization but strongly enhanced magnetic octupole and time-reversal-odd spin-splitter conductivity. We further show that the first-order transition line terminates at a quantum critical endpoint, giving rise to a rich phase diagram as the chemical potential and anisotropic hopping strength are varied. Our results identify the spin split vHSs as a route to enhancing spin-splitter effect for spintronic applications.

## 18. Collinear and Coplanar Altermagnetism in a Penrose Quasicrystal

- **提交日期**：2026-10-26
- **作者**：Bai-He Yao, Zhi-Yan Shao, Ye Cao, Fan Yang
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35855v2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35855v2)

### 摘要
We generalize the previous group-theory based classification of collinear magnetism in the quasicrystal (QC) to general magnetic states. Taking the Penrose lattice as an example, we find that the magnetic states corresponding to 2D irreducible representations (IRRPs) of the $D\_5$ point group represent for a new type of altermagnetism (AM). Unlike conventional AM wherein zero magnetism is protected by, e.g. rotation or mirror reflection, which globally flips the spin in the magnetic pattern, the zero magnetism in these 2D-IRRP states is instead protected by a special group-representation-orthogonality theorem which states that basis functions belonging to different IRRPs are mutually orthogonal. This special compensation mechanism and the absence of $\mathcal{PT}$ symmetry finally lead to AM states. Taking the Penrose-Hubbard model as an example, our linear-response theory and Ginzburg-Landau theory based analysis show that the largest portion of the parameter space is occupied by such 2D-IRRP states, which can be either collinear or coplanar AM states. These special AM states are characterized by spin split in the spin-resolved angular-resolved photoemission spectroscopy and the spin conductance with unique symmetry features. These intriguing collinear and coplanar AM states result from symmetry and can also emerge in other QCs.

## 19. Electronic Responses in Solid Oxygen with Coexisting Structural and Magnetic Orders

- **提交日期**：2026-10-26
- **作者**：Naomichi Sato, Makoto Naka, Joji Nasu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34794v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34794v1)

### 摘要
Solid oxygen provides a unique platform for studying the interplay between structural and magnetic degrees of freedom, as each O${}\_2$ molecule possesses an $S=1$ spin and weak intermolecular van der Waals interactions allow substantial structural flexibility. In addition, because of its small intermolecular hopping integrals, this system is known to be a strongly correlated electron system with a multiorbital nature. In this study, we examine electronic states and magnetic and dielectric responses using a multiorbital Hubbard model with Peierls-type electron-lattice coupling. This model provides a minimal description of the interplay between structural and magnetic degrees of freedom in solid oxygen. We apply the Hartree-Fock approximation to obtain the ground-state phase diagram. Antiferromagnetic order appears when the onsite Coulomb interaction is strong, whereas three nonmagnetic bond-ordered states emerge when it is weak. At intermediate interaction strengths, antiferromagnetic order coexists with each of the three bond orders. These three coexistence phases all break time-reversal symmetry; one preserves spatial inversion symmetry, whereas the other two also break it. The two inversion-breaking phases exhibit linear magnetoelectric responses, whereas the inversion-symmetric phase exhibits altermagnetic spin splitting and a piezomagnetic response. These responses may provide experimental signatures of coexisting magnetic and structural orders in high-pressure solid oxygen suggested by recent experiments.
