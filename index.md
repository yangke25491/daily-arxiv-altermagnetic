## 1. Noise-Protected Superconducting Qudits from Higher-Harmonic Josephson Junctions

- **提交日期**：2026-11-12
- **作者**：Hamed Vakili
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16563v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16563v1)

### 摘要
Qudits, which generalize qubits to $d$ levels, store more information per circuit element than qubits. As with qubits, however, their sensitivity to noise must be overcome before they can be used for fault-tolerant quantum computing. Here we show that, by exploiting the second harmonic of the current--phase relation, a superconducting loop of three Josephson junctions can host qutrits strongly protected against relaxation, while dephasing limits their coherence. When the second harmonic is positive, a simple loop with a small asymmetry between the junctions provides a tunable, symmetry-protected qutrit. When it is negative, a protected qutrit can be realized by adding a small symmetry-breaking term, such as a weak Josephson diode effect. The two designs involve different trade-offs: the first has an exact charge selection rule, whereas the second is nearly insensitive to charge noise and its quasiparticle errors can be corrected. The preferable choice therefore depends on the materials used. Both require a sizable second harmonic to begin with, and we identify altermagnetic Josephson junctions as a candidate platform. Finally, microscopic calculations show that a gate voltage selects the sign of the second harmonic in these junctions, although gate-voltage noise then becomes the leading source of dephasing. These findings establish the sign of the second harmonic as a design principle for protected superconducting qudits.

## 2. Kitaev Exchange and Altermagnetism in the Double Perovskite Sr${}\_2$CoTeO${}\_6$: An Ab Initio Study

- **提交日期**：2026-11-11
- **作者**：Alexey V. Ushakov, Sergey V. Strelsov
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15152v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15152v1)

### 摘要
The Co$^{2+}$-based double perovskite Sr${}\_2$CoTeO${}\_6$ attracts attention due to its nontrivial magnetic properties. Ab initio density functional theory calculations reveal a substantial ferromagnetic Kitaev exchange $|K|\sim J/3$ between nearest neighbors that, combined with the face-centered cubic lattice, leads to pronounced exchange frustration and places Sr${}\_2$CoTeO${}\_6$ among Kitaev materials with moderate Kitaev interaction strength. In addition, Sr${}\_2$CoTeO${}\_6$ is found to be altermagnetic with a sizable non-relativistic spin splitting, which can give rise to a transverse piezomagnetic effect and a nontrivial magneto-optical response.

## 3. Higher-order topology induced by altermagnetism in quasicrystals

- **提交日期**：2026-11-11
- **作者**：Xingmin Huo, Xingchuan Zhu, Xingyu Ma, Shiping Feng, Huaiming Guo
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15139v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15139v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnetism (AM), a novel magnetic phase featuring zero net magnetization and momentum-dependent spin splitting, provides a compelling platform for exploring exotic quantum phenomena. While recent studies have extended the concept of AM to quasicrystals, the momentum-dependent spin splitting in the absence of translational symmetry remains inadequately understood. Here, we investigate the emergence of higher-order topological phases (HOTPs) in Thue-Morse and Ammann-Beenker quasicrystals, driven by the coupling between $d$-wave quasicrystal AM orders and a two-dimensional topological insulator. We reveal that the $d\_{x^2-y^2}$-wave AM exhibits a ferromagnetism-like aspect in opening edge gaps and inducing zero-energy higher-order topological corner states. In contrast, conventional antiferromagnetic and $d\_{xy}$-wave AM configurations are unable to induce the higher-order topological gaps. These findings highlight the pivotal role of altermagnetic symmetry and its alignment with lattice corners in the realization of HOTPs and establish that the emergence of HOTPs can serve as a signature for identifying altermagnetic orders in aperiodic systems.

## 4. Hidden interfacial magnetism in RuO${}\_2$ heterostructures revealed by a superconducting spin switch

- **提交日期**：2026-11-10
- **作者**：Tetsuma Mandokoro, Hisakazu Matsuki, Shutaro Karube, Rodrigo de las Heras, Andrei Mazanik, Akihiro Yamada, Fugo Tokoro, Susumu Hirata, Ryusuke Hisatomi, Yoichi Shiota, Vitaly N. Golovach, F. Sebastian Bergeret, Teruo Ono
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13579v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13579v1)

### 摘要
RuO${}\_2$, once the flagship altermagnetic candidate, is now believed to be nonmagnetic in the bulk, yet weak, static and spatially inhomogeneous magnetism emerges at its surfaces and interfaces. Where this magnetism resides, what stabilises it, and whether it is connected to these states remain open questions. Here we address these questions at the Nb/RuO${}\_2$ interface using a superconducting spin switch. A GdN/Nb/RuO${}\_2$ trilayer allows interfacial exchange-field alignment to tune the superconducting transition temperature of Nb, probing interfacial magnetism inaccessible to conventional magnetometry. The switch reveals a hidden magnetic subsystem, which we attribute to a thin RuO${}\_x$ redox layer formed as Nb scavenges oxygen from RuO${}\_2$. This layer behaves as a weak and magnetically hard ferromagnet with vacancy-induced anisotropy. Furthermore, supercurrent amplifies the switching efficiency, making the probe sensitivity tunable in situ. Superconducting spin switches thus emerge as a quantitative low-temperature probe of hidden magnetism at buried interfaces.

## 5. Majorana corner modes and tunable braiding in an altermagnet-superconductor heterostructure

- **提交日期**：2026-11-05
- **作者**：Yingli Gao, Kai Zhang, Ling-Zheng Meng, Junpeng Hou, Ning Li, Ya-Jie Wu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07645v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07645v1)

### 摘要
Zeeman fields are usually required for realizing and manipulating Majorana corner modes at the cost of suppressed superconducting pairing. Altermagnetism offers a new route toward realizing Majorana corner modes under zero net external magnetic fields. Here we propose a theoretical scheme to host Majorana corner modes in a honeycomb-lattice altermagnet-superconductor heterostructure. In contrast to previous studies on square lattices, altermagnetism yields distinct effective low-energy edge masses on various inequivalent edges of the honeycomb lattice. Within a low-energy edge-theory framework, we establish the conditions under which Majorana corner modes emerge for both bearded and zigzag edge terminations. We further explicitly construct a complete set of Clifford gates by tuning the altermagnetic anisotropy and chemical potential of the heterostructure. Our results provide a promising platform for the realization and manipulation of Majorana corner modes.

## 6. LaQX: A JAX Toolkit for Neural Quantum State Simulations of Lattice Quantum Many-Body Models

- **提交日期**：2026-11-03
- **作者**：Yuntian Gu, Chaohui Fan, Ruichen Li, Haoxiang Chen, Weizhong Fu, Yixiao Chen, Dingshun Lv, Weiluo Ren, Tao Xiang, Liwei Wang, Mingpu Qin, Xiang Li
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04333v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04333v1)

### 摘要
Neural quantum states provide accurate variational representations for interacting lattice fermions and spins. Turning a new ansatz into a reproducible calculation, however, requires coordinated implementations of the Hamiltonian, sampling, optimization, observables, and accelerator parallelism. We present LaQX (Lattice Quantum simulation based on jaX), an open-source neural quantum state library based on JAX that organizes these components through common functional interfaces. Beyond ground-state variational Monte Carlo, LaQX supports excited-state calculations, Green's Function Monte Carlo refinement, adiabatic evolution, and real-time evolution with time-dependent variational Monte Carlo. It consolidates methods developed in recent high-accuracy neural quantum state studies of the Hubbard model and frustrated magnets, and the package has been used to investigate the triangular-lattice Hofstadter-Hubbard model. We document these capabilities with reproducible calculations for Hubbard, $J\_1$-$J\_2$ Heisenberg, altermagnetic Hubbard, and interacting Hofstadter models. Function-oriented modules separate the scientific components and limit the source context needed for extensions by human developers and large language models. Source code, documentation, and executable examples are available at https://github.com/bytedance/laqx.

## 7. Resolving magnetic order and dynamics with polarized neutron scattering: a practical guide to longitudinal polarization analysis

- **提交日期**：2026-11-02
- **作者**：Sijie Xu, Pengcheng Dai
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04050v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04050v1)

### 摘要
Neutron scattering provides microscopic information on magnetic order and spin dynamics. Most experiments, however, use unpolarized neutrons and do not fully exploit the information carried by the neutron spin. Longitudinal polarization analysis (LPA), established in the pioneering work of Moon, Riste, and Koehler at Oak Ridge National Laboratory in the late 1960s, separates magnetic and nonmagnetic scattering contributions through their different effects on the neutron spin. In this review, we illustrate how LPA can distinguish weak magnetic scattering from nonmagnetic contributions, constrain small moment components, and resolve the spin-space anisotropy of magnetic excitations. We introduce the formalism connecting measured polarization channels to magnetic quantities and discuss scattering geometry, crystallographic projections, and corrections for finite polarization. Our aim is to provide a practical guide for neutron scatterers using LPA to determine magnetic structures and characterize spin excitations in quantum materials. We conclude with opportunities in altermagnets, unconventional superconductors, and kagome magnets.

## 8. X-ray magnetic circular dichroism in altermagnets: insights from symmetry

- **提交日期**：2026-10-31
- **作者**：Hana Schiff, Ishmam Nihal, John Vinson, Robin Msiska, Judit Romhányi, David Lederman, Christoph Klewe, Sujoy Roy, Eric E. Fullerton
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00622v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00622v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnets are a unique subset of antiferromagnets possessing ferromagnet-like responses that are odd in the Néel vector, making X-ray magnetic circular dichroism (XMCD) a natural probe of ground state altermagnetic order. Because the relativistic ingredient of XMCD at the $L$-edge is supplied predominantly by core-level spin--orbit coupling (SOC), the dichroic signal can reflect the non-relativistic order of the valence electrons. Here we develop a complete symmetry framework for XMCD in altermagnets (AMs) preserving the crystalline unit cell. We tabulate the symmetry-allowed optical Hall vector, anisotropic magnetic dipole (AMD), and underlying octupolar components for all magnetic point groups, thereby identifying the experimental geometries that maximize the XMCD signal. We show that only twelve magnetic point groups, none with a rotation axis of order three or higher, are compatible with both altermagnetism and finite XMCD. By extending the Landau theory of collinear AMs, we connect these relativistic constraints to the spin--orbit-free order. We show that the AMD is forced to vanish at zero valence SOC in conventional antiferromagnets, and that every AM sublattice structure allows the Hall vector and AMD at linear or cubic order in the Néel vector; whether the resulting XMCD is nonzero depends on the Néel vector orientation at finite SOC. The Landau theory thus fixes the Néel-vector dependence of the XMCD for all AM candidates, and separates $d$-wave AMs, whose signal is inherited from a non-relativistic AMD, from $g$- and $i$-wave AMs, whose signal requires valence SOC or core--valence multiplet effects. We illustrate these predictions with first-principles Bethe--Salpeter calculations of $L$-edge XMCD for five representative altermagnetic materials and one conventional antiferromagnet.

## 9. Meta-Altermagnetism and Enhanced Spin-Splitting

- **提交日期**：2026-10-27
- **作者**：Bogeng Wen, Hae-Young Kee
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37754v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37754v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnets are collinear magnets with zero net magnetization and spin-split electronic bands. Here, we explore whether altermagnetism can exhibit a discontinuous jump, a phenomenon we refer to as meta-altermagnetism. We show that meta-altermagnetism can emerge in 2D systems when magnetic-order-induced band reconstruction drives spin-split van Hove singularities (vHSs) across the Fermi level. Using mean-field theory for an extended $t$-$J$ model on a two-sublattice square lattice including anisotropic hopping integral, we find that a first-order meta-altermagnetic transition produces an intermediate altermagnetic regime with reduced staggered magnetization but strongly enhanced magnetic octupole and time-reversal-odd spin-splitter conductivity. We further show that the first-order transition line terminates at a quantum critical endpoint, giving rise to a rich phase diagram as the chemical potential and anisotropic hopping strength are varied. Our results identify the spin split vHSs as a route to enhancing spin-splitter effect for spintronic applications.

## 10. Collinear and Coplanar Altermagnetism in a Penrose Quasicrystal

- **提交日期**：2026-10-26
- **作者**：Bai-He Yao, Zhi-Yan Shao, Ye Cao, Fan Yang
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35855v2](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35855v2)

### 摘要
We generalize the previous group-theory based classification of collinear magnetism in the quasicrystal (QC) to general magnetic states. Taking the Penrose lattice as an example, we find that the magnetic states corresponding to 2D irreducible representations (IRRPs) of the $D\_5$ point group represent for a new type of altermagnetism (AM). Unlike conventional AM wherein zero magnetism is protected by, e.g. rotation or mirror reflection, which globally flips the spin in the magnetic pattern, the zero magnetism in these 2D-IRRP states is instead protected by a special group-representation-orthogonality theorem which states that basis functions belonging to different IRRPs are mutually orthogonal. This special compensation mechanism and the absence of $\mathcal{PT}$ symmetry finally lead to AM states. Taking the Penrose-Hubbard model as an example, our linear-response theory and Ginzburg-Landau theory based analysis show that the largest portion of the parameter space is occupied by such 2D-IRRP states, which can be either collinear or coplanar AM states. These special AM states are characterized by spin split in the spin-resolved angular-resolved photoemission spectroscopy and the spin conductance with unique symmetry features. These intriguing collinear and coplanar AM states result from symmetry and can also emerge in other QCs.

## 11. Electronic Responses in Solid Oxygen with Coexisting Structural and Magnetic Orders

- **提交日期**：2026-10-26
- **作者**：Naomichi Sato, Makoto Naka, Joji Nasu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34794v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34794v1)

### 摘要
Solid oxygen provides a unique platform for studying the interplay between structural and magnetic degrees of freedom, as each O${}\_2$ molecule possesses an $S=1$ spin and weak intermolecular van der Waals interactions allow substantial structural flexibility. In addition, because of its small intermolecular hopping integrals, this system is known to be a strongly correlated electron system with a multiorbital nature. In this study, we examine electronic states and magnetic and dielectric responses using a multiorbital Hubbard model with Peierls-type electron-lattice coupling. This model provides a minimal description of the interplay between structural and magnetic degrees of freedom in solid oxygen. We apply the Hartree-Fock approximation to obtain the ground-state phase diagram. Antiferromagnetic order appears when the onsite Coulomb interaction is strong, whereas three nonmagnetic bond-ordered states emerge when it is weak. At intermediate interaction strengths, antiferromagnetic order coexists with each of the three bond orders. These three coexistence phases all break time-reversal symmetry; one preserves spatial inversion symmetry, whereas the other two also break it. The two inversion-breaking phases exhibit linear magnetoelectric responses, whereas the inversion-symmetric phase exhibits altermagnetic spin splitting and a piezomagnetic response. These responses may provide experimental signatures of coexisting magnetic and structural orders in high-pressure solid oxygen suggested by recent experiments.

## 12. Altermagnetism in Folded Iron Chain Systems

- **提交日期**：2026-10-22
- **作者**：Ling-Fang Lin, Yang Zhang, Satoshi Okamoto, Adriana Moreo, Lun-Hui Hu, Elbio Dagotto
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31732v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31732v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnetism, a moment-compensated magnetism with momentum-dependent spin splitting, has so far been realized primarily in Néel-type antiferromagnets. Here, we demonstrate altermagnetism in folded iron-chain systems with block-type magnetic order (e.g., $\uparrow \uparrow \downarrow \downarrow$). In the strictly one-dimensional (1D) case, altermagnetism is forbidden by the $[C\_2||{\cal T}]$ symmetry, but it can emerge when such chains are stacked into a quasi-1D array. To generate altermagnetic spin splitting via this chain-stacking mechanism, two essential ingredients are required: a block-type spin order that spontaneously breaks the combined parity-time reversal symmetry, and finite inter-chain hopping. Using first-principles calculations, we identify the K${}\_3$Fe${}\_2$Se${}\_4$ family as a promising platform that realizes this mechanism, hosting a $d$-wave altermagnet even without lattice $C\_{4}$ symmetry. Moreover, owing to its lower symmetry, the block-type folded chain exhibits an isotropic altermagnetic spin splitting, which may manifest as an altermagnetic wrapping effect. Our findings unify real-space block magnetism and reciprocal-space altermagnetism, establishing folded iron chains as a tunable platform for zero-moment spintronic applications.

## 13. Emergent altermagnetism in (LaVO${}\_3$)${}\_n$/(LaNiO${}\_3$)${}\_m$ superlattices from interfacial charge transfer and octahedral tilts

- **提交日期**：2026-10-21
- **作者**：Gustavo H. Cassemiro, Walber H. Brito
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30371v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30371v1)

### 摘要
Using first-principles calculations, we show that altermagnetism emerges in (LaVO${}\_3$)${}\_n$/(LaNiO${}\_3$)${}\_m$ superlattices, with $(n,m) = (1,1), (2,2)$ and $(2,4)$, from the interplay of interfacial charge transfer and octahedral tilts. Charge transfer drives the system towards the V$^{4+}$ and Ni$^{2+}$ configuration, turning the otherwise paramagnetic nickelate layers into a magnetically active sublattice. The resulting $C$-type antiferromagnetic order, combined with $a^-b^+c^-$ or $a^-b^-c^-$ octahedral tilts, links opposite-spin sublattices only through nonsymmorphic operations, yielding a $d$-wave spin splitting without spin-orbit coupling. Within DFT+U, the splitting reaches 118 meV in the insulating (LVO)${}\_1$/(LNO)${}\_1$ superlattice, about twice that calculated for bulk LaVO${}\_3$, and decreases as the nickelate layers become thicker. Our findings indicate that this splitting is hosted by both the V and Ni sublattices, with nearly half of its weight on oxygen $2p$ states through $p$-$d$ covalency. Interfacial charge transfer thus emerges as a design principle for engineering altermagnetism in oxide heterostructures.

## 14. Quantics tensor trains for multiloop functional renormalization group in multiorbital impurity models

- **提交日期**：2026-10-21
- **作者**：Hyun-Yong Lee, Naoki Kawashima
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29341v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29341v1)

### 摘要
The full frequency dependence of electronic interactions is central to dynamical correlations, but its numerical cost restricts multiorbital calculations. We formulate the multiloop functional renormalization group directly in the quantics tensor train representation, retaining all three independent vertex frequencies over broad ranges without an explicit high-frequency parametrization. Analytical tensor operators perform frequency transformations and internal summations without dense vertex intermediates. Multiloop corrections improve agreement of vertices and responses with independent references and reproduce the low-energy numerical renormalization group self-energy. Lower-temperature calculations resolve a quadratic frequency contribution to the analytically continued scattering rate. A degenerate two-orbital impurity exhibits distinct spin, orbital, and charge responses at fixed Hund coupling. In a compensated altermagnetic environment, an orbital-level splitting induces a local spin response despite zero magnetization of the unperturbed impurity. Increasing Hund coupling suppresses the orbital response while increasing its spin polarization. Renormalized one-particle propagation alone does not reproduce this strong polarization dependence. These results extend compressed multiloop calculations to dynamical correlations in multiorbital environments.

## 15. Engineering nonrelativistic spin splitting in semiconductors by altermagnetic proximity

- **提交日期**：2026-10-16
- **作者**：Sing Lam Wong, Marcel Franz
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23773v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23773v1)

### 摘要
Altermagnets combine vanishing net magnetization with momentum-dependent, nonrelativistic spin splitting, offering functionalities attractive for spintronics and quantum devices. Their practical use, however, is constrained by the limited availability of materials that simultaneously possess the required altermagnetic symmetry, semiconducting electronic structure, and weak spin-orbit coupling. Here we show that these requirements can instead be separated between constituent materials through proximity engineering. We develop an analytical theory for a conventional semiconductor coupled to a gapped $d$-wave altermagnet and demonstrate that virtual tunneling transfers the altermagnetic spin splitting to the semiconductor. Crucially, the induced nonrelativistic and relativistic spin splittings exhibit qualitatively different dependences on band alignment. Virtual processes involving the valence and conduction bands of the altermagnet can reinforce the altermagnetic splitting while contributing with opposite signs to the interfacial Rashba coupling. Consequently, electrostatic tuning can access a regime in which the Rashba splitting vanishes while a sizable $d$-wave spin splitting persists. Exact diagonalization of the full heterostructure model confirms the analytical theory and establishes the robustness of this mechanism beyond the perturbative regime. Our results identify altermagnetic proximity as a route for engineering nonrelativistic spin-split electronic states in established semiconductor platforms, providing a general strategy for disentangling magnetic and relativistic spin interactions in quantum heterostructures.

## 17. Hidden Staggered Superconducting Order in V2Se2O-Family Altermagnets

- **提交日期**：2026-10-16
- **作者**：Chun Yu Wan, Xiangxi Zhang, Fei Yang, Junwei Liu
- **arXiv链接**：[http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22935v1](http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22935v1)

### 摘要
$η$ pairing describes a staggered superconducting condensate whose order parameter changes sign between neighboring unit cells while maintaining a uniform magnitude. Nearly four decades after its theoretical proposal by C. N. Yang, a material platform for stabilizing $η$ pairing has remained elusive. Here, we reveal that altermagnetism, despite being pair breaking for conventional BCS pairing, naturally favors $η$-like pairing in V${}\_2$Se${}\_2$O-family altermagnets. Their valley-contrasting spin structure gives rise to oppositely spin-polarized, symmetry-related valleys centered at $X=(π,0)$ and $Y=(0,π)$, which form spin-singlet pairing partners separated by the commensurate momentum $\mathbf Q=(π,π)$, thereby favoring an $η$-like condensate. This constitutes a charge-$4e$-like Josephson response despite a bulk condensate composed of charge-$2e$ Cooper pairs, providing a direct phase-sensitive signature of the otherwise elusive $η$-like condensate in V${}\_2$Se${}\_2$O-family altermagnets.

## 18. Comment on "Skyrmion Hall Effect in Altermagnets"

- **提交日期**：2026-10-15

### 摘要

Lett. 133, 196701 (2024)] predict a transverse velocity $v\_y$ for skyrmions driven by spin-transfer torque (STT), known as skyrmion Hall effect (SkHE), in d-wave altermagnets. Usually, the SkHE emerges from the skyrmion's topological charge which here cancels between sublattices. Instead, Jin et al. attribute the SkHE to the dimensionless quadrupole tensor $A\_{ij}$.
